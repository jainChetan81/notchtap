Weather art — the shipped overlay's weather surfaces

Two distinct consumers of the same condition -> (glyph, mood, texture) table (src/lib/weatherArt.ts): the weather ALERT card — a real promoted notification, full masthead/title/body/ stamp/TTL-bar chrome with the mood art painted behind it — and the idle hover-peek's weather backdrop — the same art reused as a persistent layer behind whatever the idle peek's precedence chain (football > media > weather > timeline) is currently showing. Glyphs are Meteocons static-fill SVGs (MIT, Bas Milius) vendored locally; mood gradients are ours, CSS-only, no third-party art.

Idle hover-peek: the same art as a persistent backdrop

Hovering the idle rail (rust-derived tracking area, never CSS :hover — the overlay window is click-through) opens a below-block beneath the flanks (IdleHoverPeek.tsx). Plan 105 (Step B) split the weather scene into a backdrop layer (WeatherPeekBackdrop, pure art, z-index 0) and a content layer (.peek-content, z-index 1) so the mood/texture survives even when something else outranks the plain weather readout — a live match keeps its own scorecard visual instead (backdrop stays out then), but a now-playing media row still lets the weather art show through behind it. Precedence: football > media > weather > timeline; the day-progress timeline is the one thing that always renders, at the bottom of every variant.
