why this reads as "the CMUX card"

Three things give this template its identity, and none of them exist in Agent Board's or Football's current bespoke layouts:

  • The 3px accent stripe + stamp pill — priority is always color, never a chip, per DESIGN.html's own Law 2.
  • The masthead hairline — a small dot + uppercase kicker + a line that trails off, the same "letterhead" gesture on every card regardless of origin.
  • The manifest disclosure — one consistent way to go from "one line" to "the full message," instead of each origin inventing its own expanded layout.
