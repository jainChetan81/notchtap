0 · The notch shell — flanks, cutout, idle face, rail furniture

StatusRailCard.tsx's outer shape (plan 091 onward): a real 3-col/2-row CSS grid, .flank-left / the notch cutout (.synthetic-cutout, HUD-mode only — real notch hardware draws nothing here) / .flank-right on row 1, .below-block spanning row 2 when a card is showing. The clock (FlankClock) and the three status dots (StatusDots) are rail furniture — they persist through a showing notification in rail mode (railRevealed), not just at idle. The idle face fades in after ~4.5s of true idle. HUD mode is shown by default (the dev machine, per CLAUDE.md, is the notch-less mac mini) — toggle "Mode" above to see notch mode, where the cutout/gills/idle-face are real hardware and this app draws nothing there.

1 · Priority accent — 3px edge, stamp pill, ttl-bar fill

One channel, three readouts: the --accent custom property (set by .card-assembly.low/.medium/.high) colors the compact row's left edge, the Stamp pill, and the TTL bar's fill/done segments. Deliberately not signal-driven — a goal shares "high" with any other high-priority source.

2 · Card content — generic/agent, news, weather, live-match

NotificationBody.tsx renders one of two branches (news vs. everything else) into .compact, plus the disclosure Manifest and the TtlBar floor strip. A live-match football card (structured ESPN meta) skips all three — LiveMatchScorecard.tsx replaces .compact entirely with .notif-block. Origin/category paint (below-block wash — news-shade/src-*/wx-card) is a deliberately separate channel from the priority accent above; a card only ever carries one of the two paint systems.

3 · Compact ↔ expanded — the manifest disclosure

expand/collapse is a CSS grid-template-rows 0fr → 1fr transition (manifest.css) — the panel stays mounted at zero height when collapsed, never remounted/re-tokenized on toggle. Toggle the button below.

4 · TTL bar — rotation countdown + queue segmentation

The 2026-07-24 "stories merge" folded the old separate queue slider (Track.tsx) into this one floor strip — TtlBar.tsx now takes total/done directly and segments the same bar the countdown fill drains across. Segment count caps at 10; beyond that the current index maps proportionally.

5 · Celebrations

Two families, gated mutually exclusive by isLiveCard (StatusRailCard.tsx): the generic pulse-goal/ pulse-red pair (confetti burst + ring + three staggered ripples, or a two-step coral strobe) for any non-football-structured source, and the live-match cele-goal/cele-yc/cele-rc family (a softer glow + ring, scoped to LiveMatchScorecard) for a real ESPN-structured goal/card. Replay each below.

6 · News categories

Seven category shaders (science split off from tech by plan 147) driving the masthead dot, category chip, and the ambient news-shade drift wash — same --cat/--cat-deep/ --cat-pill triple every card's below-block reads.

7 · Source identity — agent runtimes + non-agent origins

Plan 147: every notification SOURCE gets its own identity colour on the below-block wash + hairline (sourceClass, source-identity.css) — a system distinct from news's per-category colour above; a card only ever uses one of the two. Agent runtime names appear neutrally per CLAUDE.md's naming section (compatibility requires them).

8 · Idle hover-peek

IdleHoverPeek.tsx — mounted only while genuinely hovered (open), never just because ambient data exists. Precedence: a live football match > now-playing media > weather > the bare day-progress timeline alone. Real crest/media artwork isn't available to a static file, so the scorecard reveal falls back to team abbreviations and the media row uses a placeholder glyph — the same fallback paths the shipped app itself takes when no cached asset exists.

9 · Bare (minimal notch) vs rail, hover reveal

resting_state: "notch" (Settings → Appearance) paints zero app pixels at idle — cutout-width-only, transparent flanks — until hovered, when it widens back to the full rail and paints the flanks black again (Task 1.2, 2026-07-23). resting_state: "rail" (the default) skips the bare/minimal look entirely and always shows the full clock+dots rail.
